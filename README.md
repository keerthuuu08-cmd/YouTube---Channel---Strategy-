## Project Overview
This project presents a YouTube channel strategy for Netflix to increase brand awareness, audience engagement, and subscriber growth.

## Objectives
- Increase YouTube subscribers
- Promote Netflix original content
- Improve audience engagement
- Increase watch time

## Target Audience
- Teenagers and Adults (16–45)
- Movie and Web Series Lovers
- Students
- Working Professionals

## Content Strategy
- Official Trailers
- Teasers
- Behind the Scenes
- Cast Interviews
- YouTube Shorts
- Trending Clips

## Upload Schedule

| Day | Content |
|------|---------|
| Monday | Official Trailer |
| Wednesday | YouTube Shorts |
| Friday | Cast Interview |
| Sunday | Behind the Scenes |

## Expected Results
- More Subscribers
- Higher Engagement
- Better Brand Awareness
- Increased Watch Time

## Conclusion
A well-planned YouTube strategy helps Netflix reach more viewers, improve engagement, and promote its latest content effectively.
